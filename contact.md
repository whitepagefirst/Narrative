## About Us

This is an online publication, built by multiple individuals who come together to create a platform
for sharing knowledge. The patronage of this site will change from time to time, and different companies will be in
charge of maintaining and improving this site to ensure its continued growth and success.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or feedback about our site, the best way to contact us is via our
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/whitepagefirst/Narrative/issues). Our team of developers and contributors are always happy to hear
from you and look forward to collaborating with you on future projects.

## Get Involved

If you're interested in contributing to our site or helping us improve it, we invite you to join our community on
GitHub. We're always looking for talented individuals to help us build and maintain this site.
